## Transparent, Pure, and Privacy-First

- **No Watermarks & No Time Limits**: Whether you record a 15-second snippet or a 2-hour workshop, Recordly never burns watermarks into your video and imposes no arbitrary time gates.
- **100% Local and Offline**: Every video frame, audio track, and project file stays strictly on your computer at `~/Recordly/projects/`. Nothing is ever transmitted to the cloud.
- **Open Source Community**: Governed openly under the MIT license. Transparent code, community-driven development, and free forever.
